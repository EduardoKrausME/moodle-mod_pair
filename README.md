# mod_pair - Pair

Pair is a Moodle activity for forming pairs of students inside a course. It is useful for peer exercises, partner work,
review activities and any course task where participants need to be organized in groups of two without creating Moodle
groups manually.

## How it works

Students can choose another participant who is currently available, while teachers can also create pairs automatically
from everyone who is still unpaired. Existing pairs are preserved when random pairing runs, and when the number of
available participants is odd one person remains unpaired.

## Pairing modes

The activity can work in three modes:

- Students choose: each participant selects an available classmate.
- Random pairing: the teacher generates pairs from the currently unpaired participants.
- Both: students may choose partners and the teacher may randomize whoever remains.

## Teacher controls

Teachers can define opening and closing dates, decide whether students may leave an existing pair and use a report that
shows all current pairs together with students who are still unpaired. Removing a pair makes both participants available
again.

## Student experience

A participant who already belongs to a pair sees the partner's name and profile picture. When changes are allowed, the
participant can leave the pair and choose again. In random-only mode, unpaired students wait until the teacher runs the
pairing process.

The plugin does not require an external service or third-party account.
